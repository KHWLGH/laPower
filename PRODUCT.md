# laPower

## Register

product

## Users and Purpose

Help people measure USB power and inspect USB-PD traffic using supported WITRN and POWER-Z meters. Make it easy to monitor live readings, review captured trends, and export measurements from a local desktop app.

## Brand Personality

Precise, quiet, and practical. Use the established Fluent desktop controls and glass appearance without competing with readings and traces.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboards, misleading smoothed data, and overlays that obscure measurements.

## Design Principles

- Separate visual preferences from recorded samples and exported values.
- Keep live monitoring and long-record review in the same workspace.
- Keep controls compact, with clear units and predictable interactions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use translated control names, visible keyboard focus, and units alongside channel colors. Preserve both themes, UI scaling, and reduced-motion behavior.
